# Venue decision under a Web of Science constraint — 2026-08-28

Ali has ruled out Scopus-only. The target must be **SCIE or SSCI** (AHCI at 20
points is acceptable but scores below an SSCI Q1). Seventeen indexed venues were
assessed on three axes: Clarivate index status, realistic
submission-to-**publication** lag measured from real 2025–26 articles, and honest
scope fit. What follows is the result and its one decisive discovery.

---

## The discovery: the deadline is soft, and that changes the answer

I checked this myself against `uak.gov.tr/page/docentlik-basvuru-sartlari-kLPHX`.
**ÜAK runs two application periods a year, March and October**, listed unbroken
on that page since 2018:

> 2018 Nisan / 2018 Ekim · 2019 Mart / 2019 Ekim · 2020 Mart / 2020 Ekim ·
> 2022 Mart / 2022 Ekim · 2023 Mart / 2023 Ekim · 2024 Mart / 2024 Ekim ·
> 2025 Mart / 2025 Ekim · **2026 Mart**

(2026 Ekim is not yet posted, which is normal — periods appear closer to the
time. 2021 is absent from the list.)

**So missing March 2027 costs roughly seven months, not the paper's score.**
That single fact is worth more than any ranking below, because every ranking
below assumes the deadline is what prices the trade — and if a second period
exists, it mostly does not.

**Verify this before relying on it.** If there is an external reason March 2027
is terminal — a contract date, an age or time limit, an institutional deadline —
say so, because the recommendation inverts.

June, September, December) and no issue between December and April, so March 2027
would require the December 2026 issue, assembled from postings around now.

## Eliminated, and why

**On the index constraint:** only **Quantitative Science Studies** — ESCI only,
verified twice with a passing positive control. Ten points, identical to the
Scopus outcome already rejected. The indexing constraint eliminates almost
nothing; **timing and scope do all the work.**

**On scope**, each of these is properly indexed and would still desk-reject:
Journal of Informetrics (scope enumerates six bibliometric topics, the paper is
none of them) · Information Processing & Management (requires a computing or
IR/NLP contribution the paper does not make) · Royal Society Open Science (no
social-science or humanities section exists) · Review of International
Organizations (prioritises new theory plus a test of it) · Public Administration
Review (domestic practitioner audience; also 8,000 words *including* references) ·
Big Data & Society (critical data studies; zero difference-in-differences papers
in 565 articles since 2022 — it could not competently review RQ2) · Scientometrics
(object is *scientific* information; survives only under a full reframe).

## The choice, stated as a trade

### If March 2027 is terminal → **PLOS ONE, submit within days**

- **SCIE, Q2 in Multidisciplinary Sciences → 20 points** (single-authored, full
  score). *The quartile is secondary-sourced; confirm it, since Q1 would make it
  30 and Q3 would make it 15.*
- **Accept-to-publication 0.82 months**, with volume, article number and DOI
  assigned on day one. **No early-access limbo** — which matters more than speed,
  because early access is the exact state ÜAK excludes by name.
- Explicit null-results policy, explicit preregistration recognition, **no length
  limit**, and a third-party-data provision that fits the IMF restriction.
- **Probability: roughly a coin flip conditional on acceptance (~45%);
  unconditionally perhaps one in four.**
- **Costs:** USD 2,477, mandatory, no subscription route, Türkiye not
  Research4Life-eligible. No disciplinary readership in institutional-discourse
  history — the paper buys a countable line and forfeits an audience. A bad
  reviewer draw (the sampled tail case ran 17.8 months) ends the attempt outright.

### If a second period exists → **Humanities and Social Sciences Communications, targeting October 2027**

- **SSCI + AHCI, JIF 4.8 (2025) — I verified the JIF on the journal's own page;
  the index listing is as reported by the research pass and not independently
  confirmed by me.** Q1 in Social Sciences Interdisciplinary → **30 points.**
- **Best scope fit of any venue for the paper as written**, with published policy
  covering both negative results and academically justified replication — the two
  things this paper most needs a venue to tolerate.
- Median 12.0–14.1 months. Submitted around 2026-09 that lands 2027-09 to
  2027-11, which straddles an October 2027 period rather than clearing it
  comfortably. **Submitting sooner is worth more here than anywhere else.**
- APC £1,390 / $1,990 / €1,590, priced at acceptance. Q1–Q3 status means the
  fee carries no ÜAK predatory-rule exposure (`UAK_RULES_VERBATIM_20260828.md`).

**The trade in one line: 20 points at about even odds in March, against 30 points
at good odds in October.** With a twice-yearly cycle, the second is the better
expected value by a wide margin. Without one, it is worthless.

## What I recommend, and what I am not deciding

Confirm the period question first — it is a single check and it dominates
everything else. If periods are twice yearly as the ÜAK page shows, **target
HSSC** and submit as early as September, accepting an October 2027 application.

The one option that is strictly worse than both: submitting to a slow
high-points venue *and* treating March 2027 as the plan. That combination gets
neither the points nor the date.

**Not decided here:** whether the paper's framing changes with the venue.
`FRAMING_OPTION_B_20260828.md` drafts an RQ1-led alternative; under HSSC the
scope-fit argument favours it, under PLOS ONE framing barely matters. That is
still Ali's call and the third-eye reviewer has been asked to adjudicate it.
