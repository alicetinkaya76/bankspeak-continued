Dear PLOS ONE Editors,

Please consider my manuscript, **"Reconstructing Bankspeak: Eight Decades of
World Bank Language, a Corpus-Selection Effect, and an Unconfirmed 2023-2025
Differential Shift,"** for publication as a Research Article.

The paper reports a preregistered test that returned no confirmed effect,
alongside two positive results that do not depend on it.

**First, a reconstruction.** Moretti and Pestre's widely cited *Bankspeak*
(2015) was published without its corpus, feature definitions or assembly rules,
so it has been discussed far more often than checked. I rebuild it from primary
World Bank documents and extend the assembled Annual Report series through
fiscal 2024. The trajectories recover qualitatively. This is a reconstruction,
not a replication, and the manuscript says so throughout.

**Second, a measurement result I did not go looking for.** Over the same fiscal
years the same archive yields a 43% or a 14% decline in temporal anchoring, and
within the two reconstructed corpora that difference is attributable to which
document series are included: the excluded sibling-organisation class rises
while the Bank's own volumes fall. That rise turns out to be a change of
membership between the two windows rather than a change in anyone's prose,
which the paper reports because an earlier draft of mine said otherwise. The magnitude is not a point estimate but a
range, −24% to −59%, depending on which components count as an Annual Report and
whether eras are weighted by year or by tokens. Earlier drafts of my own
attributed the gap to the wrong operation. The correction is in the paper
because it is the more useful finding.

**Third, the preregistered test.** The statistical analysis plan was sealed and
externally timestamped before any outcome reported here was computed (Zenodo
`10.5281/zenodo.22098259`; Stage-A preregistration OSF `10.17605/OSF.IO/5C9J8`).
A four-condition rule over two panels did not confirm a post-2022 differential
shift against an IMF Article IV comparator. The manuscript reports why the
design cannot settle the question either: three post-period years, one
sign-flip block, ex ante power of 0.16–0.22, and a calibration study showing
that an upper bound on the rule's false-positive rate runs 0.028–0.121 depending
on a null nobody preregistered. The negative result is reported as inconclusive,
not as evidence of absence.

**Data and code.** Analysis code and derived inputs are archived at Zenodo
`10.5281/zenodo.22277902`. IMF Article IV documents are third-party
material and are not redistributed. The manuscript publishes the full eligible
annual frame, the sampling rule, per-cell seeds and inclusion probabilities so
the sample can be redrawn independently. [Evidence deposit DOI or reviewer
