Analysis code, test suite and frozen design record behind the PLOS ONE submission of *Reconstructing Bankspeak*.

This is the release the manuscript cites. It supersedes v1.2.0, which predates the joint Holm calibration (S10.4), the sibling-class decomposition (Table 3d), the functional-form and block-origin sensitivities with time order kept (Tables 5c and 5d), the per-year unit inventory (S10.9), the submission guards, and the final prose pass.

Suite: 479 tests. The evidence deposit is a separate Zenodo record. The IMF Article IV documents are not redistributed under the written permission and are listed by report number, DOI and SHA-256 in `data/meta/imf_document_index.csv`.
