Analysis code, test suite and frozen design record behind the PLOS ONE submission of *Reconstructing Bankspeak*.

This is the release the manuscript cites. It supersedes v1.3.0, whose tag was cut at the round-19 commit (0d3ba98) rather than at the head of main, so the v1.3.0 archive (10.5281/zenodo.22271589) predates the joint Holm calibration (S10.4), the sibling-class decomposition (Table 3d), the functional-form and block-origin sensitivities with time order kept (Tables 5c and 5d), the per-year unit inventory (S10.9), the submission guards, the final prose pass and the author fields. Do not cite v1.2.0 or v1.3.0 for the results reported in the manuscript.

Suite: 479 tests. The evidence deposit is a separate Zenodo record. The IMF Article IV documents are not redistributed under the written permission and are listed by report number, DOI and SHA-256 in `data/meta/imf_document_index.csv`.
